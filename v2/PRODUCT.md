# Product

Personal portfolio for Rayyan Syed, CS (Engineering) student at Penn State, graduating May 2027.

## Mode
Experience (portfolio). The work leads; the interface recedes.

## Audience
Recruiters and engineers for AI / full-stack software internships and new-grad roles. They skim for 30 seconds: who is this, what has been shipped, how to reach them.

## Success
Visitor sees 5 real projects with concrete outcomes within one scroll, and finds email, GitHub and LinkedIn immediately.

## Visual world
Daybreak design system (claude.ai artifact B3UtytWBETXCfYbmf9RbBW): paper pages, Instrument Serif / Geist / Geist Mono, sage accent, one backdrop family. Chosen family: painted dusk scene (drawn in SVG, with fireflies as a nod to the FireFly project). Used in hero and closing section only.

## Constraints
Light rough draft. Static site, no build step, under ~100KB excluding fonts. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and dark mode.

## Assumptions (no interview was possible)
- Resume phone number stays off the public site.
- Project claims come only from the resume and repo READMEs; no invented awards or metrics.
